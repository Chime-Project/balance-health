Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 14, 2026 · Last Updated: August 19, 2026

Balance Health (“Balance Health,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of the information entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when individuals visit our website, join our early access program, communicate with our team, or use services made available through Balance Health.

When applicable, protected health information (“PHI”) is handled in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (“HIPAA”) and our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

1. About Balance Health

Balance Health is a healthcare technology and telehealth platform designed to connect individuals with wellness support, licensed healthcare providers, pharmacy partners, laboratory services, and related healthcare resources.

Availability of services may vary by state, provider licensure, and applicable laws and regulations.

We may use collected information to:

4. Calls, Text Messages, and Electronic Communications

By providing a telephone number, you consent to receive communications from Balance Health and authorized service providers.

Communications may include:

  • Phone Calls
  • SMS Text Messages communications:
  • Email Communications
  • Appointment Reminders
  • Administrative Notifications
  • Service Announcements
  • Early Access Updates

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

Users may opt out of SMS communications by replying STOP to any text message, where applicable.

If you provide your mobile telephone number and opt in to receive SMS or text messages from Balance Health Group, we may use your mobile number to send you communications consistent with the consent you provided. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS/text communications at any time by replying STOP to a message. You may also contact us to update your mobile number or communication preferences.

SMS opt-in consent, mobile telephone numbers, and related SMS consent data will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may disclose information to service providers that help us deliver SMS communications on our behalf, but only as necessary to provide those services and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

5. AI-Assisted Communications

Balance Health may utilize artificial intelligence (“AI”) technologies and automated systems for certain administrative and customer-support functions.

Examples may include:

  • Scheduling Assistance
  • Appointment Coordination
  • Customer Support
  • Follow-Up Communications
  • Educational Content Delivery
  • Administrative Intake Assistance

AI-assisted systems do not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medications, determine treatment eligibility, or replace licensed healthcare professionals.

Clinical decisions are made exclusively by licensed healthcare providers.

Certain information collected through Balance Health may qualify as Protected Health Information (“PHI”) under HIPAA.

When HIPAA applies, Balance Health uses and discloses PHI only as permitted by applicable federal and state laws.

Additional details are available in our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

  • Request an accounting of certain disclosures of protected health information, where applicable.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information or consumer health data where permitted by applicable law.
